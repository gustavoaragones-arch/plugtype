Can you use your devices in Ivory Coast?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Ivory Coast once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Ivory Coast?

Yes — if your plug type from Saint Lucia does not match the outlets in Ivory Coast, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Ivory Coast—they are not always the same as in Saint Lucia.

Why is this different?

Saint Lucia uses 230V electricity, while Ivory Coast uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
