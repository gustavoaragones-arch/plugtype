Can you use your devices in Saint Kitts and Nevis?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Saint Kitts and Nevis once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Kitts and Nevis?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Saint Kitts and Nevis, you will still need a travel adapter.

Saint Kitts and Nevis uses plug types A B G, which may differ from Suriname.

Compatibility summary

Your Suriname plug may fit in Saint Kitts and Nevis outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Suriname uses 110V electricity, while Saint Kitts and Nevis uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
