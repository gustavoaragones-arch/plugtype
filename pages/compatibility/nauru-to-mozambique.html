Can you use your devices in Mozambique?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Mozambique once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Mozambique?

Yes — if your plug type from Nauru does not match the outlets in Mozambique, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F M N in Mozambique—they are not always the same as in Nauru.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Mozambique; plug types used there differ from Nauru.

Plug comparison

Nauru plug types: I

Mozambique plug types: C F M N

There is no common plug type between Nauru (I) and Mozambique (C F M N). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Nauru: 240V, 50Hz

Mozambique: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Nauru uses 240V electricity, while Mozambique uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
