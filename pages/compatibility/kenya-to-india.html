Can you use your devices in India?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in India with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for India?

Yes — if your plug type from Kenya does not match the outlets in India, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C D M in India—they are not always the same as in Kenya.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in India; plug types used there differ from Kenya.

Voltage comparison

Kenya: 240V, 50Hz

India: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Kenya uses 240V electricity, while India uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
