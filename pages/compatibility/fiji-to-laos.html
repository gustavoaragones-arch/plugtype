Can you use your devices in Laos?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Laos once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Laos?

Yes — if your plug type from Fiji does not match the outlets in Laos, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types A B C in Laos—they are not always the same as in Fiji.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Laos; plug types used there differ from Fiji.

Plug comparison

Fiji plug types: I

Laos plug types: A B C

There is no common plug type between Fiji (I) and Laos (A B C). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Fiji: 240V, 50Hz

Laos: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Fiji uses 240V electricity, while Laos uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
