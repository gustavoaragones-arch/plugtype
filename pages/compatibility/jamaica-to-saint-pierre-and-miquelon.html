Can you use your devices in Saint Pierre and Miquelon?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Saint Pierre and Miquelon once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Pierre and Miquelon?

Yes — if your plug type from Jamaica does not match the outlets in Saint Pierre and Miquelon, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Saint Pierre and Miquelon—they are not always the same as in Jamaica.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Saint Pierre and Miquelon; plug types used there differ from Jamaica. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Jamaica uses 110V electricity, while Saint Pierre and Miquelon uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
