Can you use your devices in Pakistan?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Pakistan—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Pakistan?

Yes — if your plug type from Ghana does not match the outlets in Pakistan, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Pakistan are built for plug types C D; compare those with what you use in Ghana.

Why is this different?

Ghana uses 230V electricity, while Pakistan uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
