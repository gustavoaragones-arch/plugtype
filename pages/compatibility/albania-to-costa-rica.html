Can you use your devices in Costa Rica?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Costa Rica—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Costa Rica?

Yes — if your plug type from Albania does not match the outlets in Costa Rica, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Costa Rica are built for plug types A B; compare those with what you use in Albania.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Costa Rica; plug types used there differ from Albania. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Albania uses 230V electricity, while Costa Rica uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
