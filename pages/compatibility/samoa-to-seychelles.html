Can you use your devices in Seychelles?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Seychelles with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Seychelles?

Yes — if your plug type from Samoa does not match the outlets in Seychelles, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Seychelles are built for plug types G; compare those with what you use in Samoa.

Voltage comparison

Samoa: 230V, 50Hz

Seychelles: 240V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Samoa uses 230V electricity, while Seychelles uses 240V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
