Can you use your devices in Andorra?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Andorra with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Andorra?

Yes — if your plug type from New Zealand does not match the outlets in Andorra, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Andorra—they are not always the same as in New Zealand.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Andorra; plug types used there differ from New Zealand.

Plug comparison

New Zealand plug types: I

Andorra plug types: C F

There is no common plug type between New Zealand (I) and Andorra (C F). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

New Zealand: 230V, 50Hz

Andorra: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

New Zealand uses 230V electricity, while Andorra uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
