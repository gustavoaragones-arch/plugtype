Can you use your devices in Uganda?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Uganda with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Uganda?

Yes — if your plug type from Vietnam does not match the outlets in Uganda, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Uganda are built for plug types G; compare those with what you use in Vietnam.

Voltage comparison

Vietnam: 220V, 50Hz

Uganda: 240V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Vietnam uses 220V electricity, while Uganda uses 240V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
