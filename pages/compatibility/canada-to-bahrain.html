Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Bahrain; plug types used there differ from Canada. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Canada plug types: A B

Bahrain plug types: G

There is no common plug type between Canada (A B) and Bahrain (G). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Canada: 120V, 60Hz

Bahrain: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Canada 120V vs Bahrain 230V).

Why is this different?

Canada uses 120V electricity, while Bahrain uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
