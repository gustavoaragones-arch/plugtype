Can you use your devices in Tunisia?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Tunisia once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Tunisia?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Tunisia, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Tunisia—they are not always the same as in Myanmar.

Compatibility summary

Your Myanmar plug may fit in Tunisia outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Plug comparison

Myanmar plug types: A B C D E F G I

Tunisia plug types: C E

Both countries share at least one plug type (A B C D E F G I and C E), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Myanmar: 230V, 50Hz

Tunisia: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Myanmar uses 230V electricity, while Tunisia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
