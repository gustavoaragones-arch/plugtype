Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Ecuador; plug types used there differ from Montenegro. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Montenegro plug types: C F

Ecuador plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Montenegro (C F) and Ecuador (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Montenegro: 230V, 50Hz

Ecuador: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Montenegro 230V vs Ecuador 120V).

Why is this different?

Montenegro uses 230V electricity, while Ecuador uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
