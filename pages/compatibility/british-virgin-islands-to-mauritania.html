Can you use your devices in Mauritania?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Mauritania—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Mauritania?

Yes — if your plug type from British Virgin Islands does not match the outlets in Mauritania, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Mauritania—they are not always the same as in British Virgin Islands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Mauritania; plug types used there differ from British Virgin Islands. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

British Virgin Islands: 110V, 60Hz

Mauritania: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (British Virgin Islands 110V vs Mauritania 230V).

Why is this different?

British Virgin Islands uses 110V electricity, while Mauritania uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
