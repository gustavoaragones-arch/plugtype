Can you use your devices in Tonga?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Tonga once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Tonga?

Yes — if your plug type from Indonesia does not match the outlets in Tonga, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types I in Tonga—they are not always the same as in Indonesia.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Tonga; plug types used there differ from Indonesia.

Plug comparison

Indonesia plug types: C F

Tonga plug types: I

There is no common plug type between Indonesia (C F) and Tonga (I). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Indonesia: 230V, 50Hz

Tonga: 240V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Indonesia uses 230V electricity, while Tonga uses 240V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
