Can you use your devices in Samoa?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Samoa—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Samoa?

Yes — if your plug type from Finland does not match the outlets in Samoa, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types I in Samoa—they are not always the same as in Finland.

Compatibility summary

Voltage comparison

Finland: 230V, 50Hz

Samoa: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Finland uses 230V electricity, while Samoa uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
