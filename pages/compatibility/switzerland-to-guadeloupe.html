Can you use your devices in Guadeloupe?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Guadeloupe with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guadeloupe?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Guadeloupe, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Guadeloupe—they are not always the same as in Switzerland.

Compatibility summary

Your Switzerland plug may fit in Guadeloupe outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Switzerland: 230V, 50Hz

Guadeloupe: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Switzerland uses 230V electricity, while Guadeloupe uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
