Can you use your devices in Montserrat?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Montserrat with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Montserrat?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Montserrat, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Montserrat are built for plug types A B G; compare those with what you use in Guatemala.

Compatibility summary

Your Guatemala plug may fit in Montserrat outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Guatemala uses 120V electricity, while Montserrat uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
