Can you use your devices in Guyana?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Guyana—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guyana?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Guyana, you will still need a travel adapter.

Guyana uses plug types A B, which may differ from Bangladesh.

Compatibility summary

Your Bangladesh plug may fit in Guyana outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Bangladesh: 230V, 50Hz

Guyana: 220V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Bangladesh uses 230V electricity, while Guyana uses 220V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
