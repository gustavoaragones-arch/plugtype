Can you use your devices in Turks and Caicos Islands?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Turks and Caicos Islands—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Turks and Caicos Islands?

Yes — if your plug type from Sweden does not match the outlets in Turks and Caicos Islands, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Turks and Caicos Islands are built for plug types A B; compare those with what you use in Sweden.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Turks and Caicos Islands; plug types used there differ from Sweden. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Sweden plug types: C F

Voltage comparison

Sweden: 230V, 50Hz

Turks and Caicos Islands: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Sweden 230V vs Turks and Caicos Islands 120V).

Why is this different?

Sweden uses 230V electricity, while Turks and Caicos Islands uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
