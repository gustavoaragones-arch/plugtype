Can you use your devices in Brazil?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Brazil with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Brazil?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Brazil, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C N in Brazil—they are not always the same as in São Tomé and Príncipe.

Compatibility summary

Your São Tomé and Príncipe plug may fit in Brazil outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

São Tomé and Príncipe uses 230V electricity, while Brazil uses 127V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
