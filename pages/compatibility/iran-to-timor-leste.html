Can you use your devices in Timor-Leste?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Timor-Leste with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Timor-Leste?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Timor-Leste, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Timor-Leste are built for plug types C E F I; compare those with what you use in Iran.

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Iran uses 230V electricity, while Timor-Leste uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
