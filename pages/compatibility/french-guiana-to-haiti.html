Can you use your devices in Haiti?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Haiti once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Haiti?

Yes — if your plug type from French Guiana does not match the outlets in Haiti, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Haiti are built for plug types A B; compare those with what you use in French Guiana.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Haiti; plug types used there differ from French Guiana. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

French Guiana uses 230V electricity, while Haiti uses 110V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
