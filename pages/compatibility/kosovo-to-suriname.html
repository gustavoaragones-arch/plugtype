Can you use your devices in Suriname?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Suriname with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Suriname?

Yes — if your plug type from Kosovo does not match the outlets in Suriname, you will need a travel adapter.

Suriname uses plug types A B, which may differ from Kosovo.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Suriname; plug types used there differ from Kosovo. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Kosovo uses 230V electricity, while Suriname uses 110V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
