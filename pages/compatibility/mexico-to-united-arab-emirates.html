Can you use your devices in United Arab Emirates?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in United Arab Emirates with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for United Arab Emirates?

Yes — if your plug type from Mexico does not match the outlets in United Arab Emirates, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in United Arab Emirates are built for plug types G; compare those with what you use in Mexico.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in United Arab Emirates; plug types used there differ from Mexico. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Mexico uses 127V electricity, while United Arab Emirates uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
