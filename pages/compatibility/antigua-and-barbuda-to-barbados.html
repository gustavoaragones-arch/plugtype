Compatibility summary

Your Antigua and Barbuda plug may fit in Barbados outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Antigua and Barbuda plug types: A B

Barbados plug types: A B

Both countries share at least one plug type (A B and A B), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Antigua and Barbuda: 230V, 60Hz

Barbados: 115V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Antigua and Barbuda 230V vs Barbados 115V).

Why is this different?

Antigua and Barbuda uses 230V electricity, while Barbados uses 115V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
