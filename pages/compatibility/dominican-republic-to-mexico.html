Can you use your devices in Mexico?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Mexico once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Mexico?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Mexico, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types A B in Mexico—they are not always the same as in Dominican Republic.

Compatibility summary

Your Dominican Republic plug may fit in Mexico outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Dominican Republic: 120V, 60Hz

Mexico: 127V, 60Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Dominican Republic uses 120V electricity, while Mexico uses 127V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
