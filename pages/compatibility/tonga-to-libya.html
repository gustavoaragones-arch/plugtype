Can you use your devices in Libya?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Libya with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Libya?

Yes — if your plug type from Tonga does not match the outlets in Libya, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F L in Libya—they are not always the same as in Tonga.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Libya; plug types used there differ from Tonga.

Plug comparison

Tonga plug types: I

Libya plug types: C F L

There is no common plug type between Tonga (I) and Libya (C F L). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Tonga: 240V, 50Hz

Libya: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Tonga uses 240V electricity, while Libya uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
