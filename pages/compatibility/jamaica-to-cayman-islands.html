Compatibility summary

Your Jamaica plug may fit in Cayman Islands outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Plug comparison

Jamaica plug types: A B

Cayman Islands plug types: A B

Both countries share at least one plug type (A B and A B), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Jamaica: 110V, 50Hz

Cayman Islands: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Jamaica uses 110V electricity, while Cayman Islands uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
