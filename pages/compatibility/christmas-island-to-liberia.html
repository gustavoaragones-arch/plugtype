Can you use your devices in Liberia?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Liberia with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Liberia?

Yes — if your plug type from Christmas Island does not match the outlets in Liberia, you will need a travel adapter.

Liberia uses plug types C D F G, which may differ from Christmas Island.

Why is this different?

Christmas Island uses 230V electricity, while Liberia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
