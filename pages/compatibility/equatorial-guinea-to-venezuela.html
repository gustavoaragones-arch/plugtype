Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Venezuela; plug types used there differ from Equatorial Guinea. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Equatorial Guinea plug types: C E

Venezuela plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Equatorial Guinea (C E) and Venezuela (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Equatorial Guinea: 230V, 50Hz

Venezuela: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Equatorial Guinea 230V vs Venezuela 120V).

Why is this different?

Equatorial Guinea uses 230V electricity, while Venezuela uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
