Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Syria; plug types used there differ from Dominican Republic. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Dominican Republic plug types: A B

Syria plug types: C E F L

There is no common plug type between Dominican Republic (A B) and Syria (C E F L). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Dominican Republic: 120V, 60Hz

Syria: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Dominican Republic 120V vs Syria 230V).

Why is this different?

Dominican Republic uses 120V electricity, while Syria uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
