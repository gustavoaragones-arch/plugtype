Can you use your devices in Honduras?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Honduras—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Honduras?

Yes — if your plug type from Turkmenistan does not match the outlets in Honduras, you will need a travel adapter.

Honduras uses plug types A B, which may differ from Turkmenistan.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Honduras; plug types used there differ from Turkmenistan. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Turkmenistan uses 230V electricity, while Honduras uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
