Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in British Virgin Islands; plug types used there differ from Turkmenistan. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Turkmenistan plug types: C F

British Virgin Islands plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Turkmenistan (C F) and British Virgin Islands (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Turkmenistan: 230V, 50Hz

British Virgin Islands: 110V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Turkmenistan 230V vs British Virgin Islands 110V).

Why is this different?

Turkmenistan uses 230V electricity, while British Virgin Islands uses 110V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
