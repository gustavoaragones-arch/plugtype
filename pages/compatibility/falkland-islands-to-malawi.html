Can you use your devices in Malawi?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Malawi with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Malawi?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Malawi, you will still need a travel adapter.

Malawi uses plug types G, which may differ from Falkland Islands.

Compatibility summary

Your Falkland Islands plug may fit in Malawi outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Falkland Islands: 240V, 50Hz

Malawi: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Falkland Islands uses 240V electricity, while Malawi uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
