Can you use your devices in Portugal?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Portugal once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Portugal?

Yes — if your plug type from Malta does not match the outlets in Portugal, you will need a travel adapter.

Portugal uses plug types C F, which may differ from Malta.

Why is this different?

Malta uses 230V electricity, while Portugal uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
