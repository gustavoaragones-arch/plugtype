Can you use your devices in Andorra?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Andorra with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Andorra?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Andorra, you will still need a travel adapter.

Andorra uses plug types C F, which may differ from French Polynesia.

Compatibility summary

Your French Polynesia plug may fit in Andorra outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

French Polynesia: 230V, 60Hz

Andorra: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

French Polynesia uses 230V electricity, while Andorra uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
