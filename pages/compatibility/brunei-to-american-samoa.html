Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in American Samoa; plug types used there differ from Brunei. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Brunei plug types: G

American Samoa plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Brunei (G) and American Samoa (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Brunei: 240V, 50Hz

American Samoa: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Brunei 240V vs American Samoa 120V).

Why is this different?

Brunei uses 240V electricity, while American Samoa uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
