Can you use your devices in Chad?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Chad with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Chad?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Chad, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Chad are built for plug types C E F; compare those with what you use in Western Sahara.

Compatibility summary

Your Western Sahara plug may fit in Chad outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Western Sahara: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Western Sahara uses 230V electricity, while Chad uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
