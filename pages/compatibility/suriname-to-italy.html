Can you use your devices in Italy?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Italy—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Italy?

Yes — if your plug type from Suriname does not match the outlets in Italy, you will need a travel adapter.

Italy uses plug types C F L, which may differ from Suriname.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Italy; plug types used there differ from Suriname. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Suriname uses 110V electricity, while Italy uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
