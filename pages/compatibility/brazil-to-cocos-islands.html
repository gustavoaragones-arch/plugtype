Can you use your devices in Cocos (Keeling) Islands?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Cocos (Keeling) Islands—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Cocos (Keeling) Islands?

Yes — if your plug type from Brazil does not match the outlets in Cocos (Keeling) Islands, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Cocos (Keeling) Islands are built for plug types I; compare those with what you use in Brazil.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Cocos (Keeling) Islands; plug types used there differ from Brazil. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Brazil uses 127V electricity, while Cocos (Keeling) Islands uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
