Can you use your devices in Cyprus?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Cyprus with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Cyprus?

Yes — if your plug type from Bahamas does not match the outlets in Cyprus, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types G in Cyprus—they are not always the same as in Bahamas.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Cyprus; plug types used there differ from Bahamas. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Bahamas uses 120V electricity, while Cyprus uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
