Can you use your devices in Christmas Island?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Christmas Island with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Christmas Island?

Yes — if your plug type from Zambia does not match the outlets in Christmas Island, you will need a travel adapter.

Christmas Island uses plug types I, which may differ from Zambia.

Why is this different?

Zambia uses 230V electricity, while Christmas Island uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
