Can you use your devices in Montenegro?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Montenegro—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Montenegro?

Yes — if your plug type from Cyprus does not match the outlets in Montenegro, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Montenegro—they are not always the same as in Cyprus.

Why is this different?

Cyprus uses 230V electricity, while Montenegro uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
