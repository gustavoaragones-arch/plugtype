Compatibility summary

Your Peru plug may fit in Guatemala outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Peru plug types: A B C

Guatemala plug types: A B

Both countries share at least one plug type (A B C and A B), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Peru: 230V, 60Hz

Guatemala: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Peru 230V vs Guatemala 120V).

Why is this different?

Peru uses 230V electricity, while Guatemala uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
