Can you use your devices in Saint Martin?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Saint Martin once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Martin?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Saint Martin, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Saint Martin are built for plug types C E; compare those with what you use in Eswatini.

Why is this different?

Eswatini uses 230V electricity, while Saint Martin uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
