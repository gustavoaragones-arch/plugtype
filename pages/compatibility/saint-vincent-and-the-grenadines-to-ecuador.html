Compatibility summary

Your Saint Vincent and the Grenadines plug may fit in Ecuador outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines plug types: A B G

Ecuador plug types: A B

Both countries share at least one plug type (A B G and A B), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: 230V, 50Hz

Ecuador: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Saint Vincent and the Grenadines 230V vs Ecuador 120V).

Why is this different?

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines uses 230V electricity, while Ecuador uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
