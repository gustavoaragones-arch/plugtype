Can you use your devices in Greenland?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Greenland—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Greenland?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Greenland, you will still need a travel adapter.

Greenland uses plug types C F K, which may differ from Niger.

Compatibility summary

Your Niger plug may fit in Greenland outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Niger: 230V, 50Hz

Greenland: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Niger uses 230V electricity, while Greenland uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
