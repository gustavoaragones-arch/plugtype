Can you use your devices in Turks and Caicos Islands?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Turks and Caicos Islands with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Turks and Caicos Islands?

Yes — if your plug type from Netherlands does not match the outlets in Turks and Caicos Islands, you will need a travel adapter.

Turks and Caicos Islands uses plug types A B, which may differ from Netherlands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Turks and Caicos Islands; plug types used there differ from Netherlands. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Voltage comparison

Netherlands: 230V, 50Hz

Turks and Caicos Islands: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Netherlands 230V vs Turks and Caicos Islands 120V).

Why is this different?

Netherlands uses 230V electricity, while Turks and Caicos Islands uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
