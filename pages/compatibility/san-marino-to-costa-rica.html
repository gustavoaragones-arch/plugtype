Can you use your devices in Costa Rica?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Costa Rica once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Costa Rica?

Yes — if your plug type from San Marino does not match the outlets in Costa Rica, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types A B in Costa Rica—they are not always the same as in San Marino.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Costa Rica; plug types used there differ from San Marino. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

San Marino: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (San Marino 230V vs Costa Rica 120V).

Why is this different?

San Marino uses 230V electricity, while Costa Rica uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
