Can you use your devices in Jordan?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Jordan—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Jordan?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Jordan, you will still need a travel adapter.

Jordan uses plug types C F G, which may differ from Argentina.

Compatibility summary

Your Argentina plug may fit in Jordan outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Plug comparison

Argentina plug types: C I

Jordan plug types: C F G

Both countries share at least one plug type (C I and C F G), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Argentina: 220V, 50Hz

Jordan: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Argentina uses 220V electricity, while Jordan uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
