Can you use your devices in Cayman Islands?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Cayman Islands with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Cayman Islands?

Yes — if your plug type from Timor-Leste does not match the outlets in Cayman Islands, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Cayman Islands are built for plug types A B; compare those with what you use in Timor-Leste.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Cayman Islands; plug types used there differ from Timor-Leste. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Timor-Leste: 230V, 50Hz

Why is this different?

Timor-Leste uses 230V electricity, while Cayman Islands uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
