Can you use your devices in British Virgin Islands?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in British Virgin Islands once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for British Virgin Islands?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in British Virgin Islands, you will still need a travel adapter.

British Virgin Islands uses plug types A B, which may differ from Micronesia.

Voltage comparison

Micronesia: 120V, 60Hz

British Virgin Islands: 110V, 60Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Micronesia uses 120V electricity, while British Virgin Islands uses 110V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
