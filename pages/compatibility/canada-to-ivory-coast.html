Can you use your devices in Ivory Coast?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Ivory Coast—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Ivory Coast?

Yes — if your plug type from Canada does not match the outlets in Ivory Coast, you will need a travel adapter.

Ivory Coast uses plug types C E, which may differ from Canada.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Ivory Coast; plug types used there differ from Canada. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Canada uses 120V electricity, while Ivory Coast uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
