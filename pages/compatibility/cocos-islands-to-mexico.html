Can you use your devices in Mexico?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Mexico with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Mexico?

Yes — if your plug type from Cocos (Keeling) Islands does not match the outlets in Mexico, you will need a travel adapter.

Mexico uses plug types A B, which may differ from Cocos (Keeling) Islands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Mexico; plug types used there differ from Cocos (Keeling) Islands. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Cocos (Keeling) Islands uses 230V electricity, while Mexico uses 127V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
