Can you use your devices in Central African Republic?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Central African Republic once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Central African Republic?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Central African Republic, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Central African Republic—they are not always the same as in Nepal.

Compatibility summary

Your Nepal plug may fit in Central African Republic outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Plug comparison

Nepal plug types: C D M

Central African Republic plug types: C E

Both countries share at least one plug type (C D M and C E), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Nepal: 230V, 50Hz

Central African Republic: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Nepal uses 230V electricity, while Central African Republic uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
