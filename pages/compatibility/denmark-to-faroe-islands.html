Can you use your devices in Faroe Islands?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Faroe Islands once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Faroe Islands?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Faroe Islands, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Faroe Islands are built for plug types C F K; compare those with what you use in Denmark.

Your Denmark plug may fit in Faroe Islands outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Denmark: 230V, 50Hz

Faroe Islands: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Denmark uses 230V electricity, while Faroe Islands uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
