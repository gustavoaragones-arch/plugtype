Can you use your devices in Guinea?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Guinea once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guinea?

Yes — if your plug type from Kiribati does not match the outlets in Guinea, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Guinea—they are not always the same as in Kiribati.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Guinea; plug types used there differ from Kiribati.

Plug comparison

Kiribati plug types: I

Guinea plug types: C F

There is no common plug type between Kiribati (I) and Guinea (C F). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Kiribati: 240V, 50Hz

Guinea: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Kiribati uses 240V electricity, while Guinea uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
