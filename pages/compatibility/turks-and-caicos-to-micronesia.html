Can you use your devices in Micronesia?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Micronesia—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Micronesia?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Micronesia, you will still need a travel adapter.

Micronesia uses plug types A B, which may differ from Turks and Caicos Islands.

Why is this different?

Turks and Caicos Islands uses 120V electricity, while Micronesia uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
