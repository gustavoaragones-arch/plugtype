Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Saudi Arabia; plug types used there differ from Guatemala. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Guatemala plug types: A B

Saudi Arabia plug types: G

There is no common plug type between Guatemala (A B) and Saudi Arabia (G). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Guatemala: 120V, 60Hz

Saudi Arabia: 230V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Guatemala 120V vs Saudi Arabia 230V).

Why is this different?

Guatemala uses 120V electricity, while Saudi Arabia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
