Can you use your devices in Malta?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Malta—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Malta?

Yes — if your plug type from Marshall Islands does not match the outlets in Malta, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Malta are built for plug types G; compare those with what you use in Marshall Islands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Malta; plug types used there differ from Marshall Islands. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Marshall Islands uses 120V electricity, while Malta uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
