Can you use your devices in France?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in France with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for France?

Yes — if your plug type from Solomon Islands does not match the outlets in France, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in France—they are not always the same as in Solomon Islands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in France; plug types used there differ from Solomon Islands.

Voltage comparison

Solomon Islands: 230V, 50Hz

France: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Solomon Islands uses 230V electricity, while France uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
