Can you use your devices in Norway?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Norway once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Norway?

Yes — if your plug type from British Virgin Islands does not match the outlets in Norway, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Norway—they are not always the same as in British Virgin Islands.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Norway; plug types used there differ from British Virgin Islands. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

British Virgin Islands uses 110V electricity, while Norway uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
