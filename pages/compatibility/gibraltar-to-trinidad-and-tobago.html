Can you use your devices in Trinidad and Tobago?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Trinidad and Tobago with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Trinidad and Tobago?

Yes — if your plug type from Gibraltar does not match the outlets in Trinidad and Tobago, you will need a travel adapter.

Trinidad and Tobago uses plug types A B, which may differ from Gibraltar.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Trinidad and Tobago; plug types used there differ from Gibraltar. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Voltage comparison

Gibraltar: 230V, 50Hz

Trinidad and Tobago: 115V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Gibraltar 230V vs Trinidad and Tobago 115V).

Why is this different?

Gibraltar uses 230V electricity, while Trinidad and Tobago uses 115V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
