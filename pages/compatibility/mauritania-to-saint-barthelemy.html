Can you use your devices in Saint Barthélemy?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Saint Barthélemy with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Barthélemy?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Saint Barthélemy, you will still need a travel adapter.

Saint Barthélemy uses plug types C E, which may differ from Mauritania.

Why is this different?

Mauritania uses 230V electricity, while Saint Barthélemy uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
