Can you use your devices in Zambia?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Zambia—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Zambia?

Yes — if your plug type from Antigua and Barbuda does not match the outlets in Zambia, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types G in Zambia—they are not always the same as in Antigua and Barbuda.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Zambia; plug types used there differ from Antigua and Barbuda.

Voltage comparison

Antigua and Barbuda: 230V, 60Hz

Zambia: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Antigua and Barbuda uses 230V electricity, while Zambia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
