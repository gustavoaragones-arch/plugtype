Can you use your devices in Fiji?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Fiji once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Fiji?

Yes — if your plug type from Barbados does not match the outlets in Fiji, you will need a travel adapter.

Fiji uses plug types I, which may differ from Barbados.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Fiji; plug types used there differ from Barbados. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Barbados uses 115V electricity, while Fiji uses 240V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
