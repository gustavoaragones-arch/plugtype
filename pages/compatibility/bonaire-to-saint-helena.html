Can you use your devices in Saint Helena?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Saint Helena—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Helena?

Yes — if your plug type from Bonaire does not match the outlets in Saint Helena, you will need a travel adapter.

Saint Helena uses plug types G, which may differ from Bonaire.

Why is this different?

Bonaire uses 127V electricity, while Saint Helena uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
