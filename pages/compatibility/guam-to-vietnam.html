Can you use your devices in Vietnam?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Vietnam once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Vietnam?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Vietnam, you will still need a travel adapter.

Vietnam uses plug types A C F, which may differ from Guam.

Compatibility summary

Your Guam plug may fit in Vietnam outlets, as both use at least one common plug type. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Guam uses 110V electricity, while Vietnam uses 220V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
