Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Lebanon; plug types used there differ from Aruba. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Aruba plug types: A B

Lebanon plug types: C F

There is no common plug type between Aruba (A B) and Lebanon (C F). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Aruba: 120V, 60Hz

Lebanon: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Aruba 120V vs Lebanon 230V).

Why is this different?

Aruba uses 120V electricity, while Lebanon uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
