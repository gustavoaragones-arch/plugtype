Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Nicaragua; plug types used there differ from Malta. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Malta plug types: G

Nicaragua plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Malta (G) and Nicaragua (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Malta: 230V, 50Hz

Nicaragua: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Malta 230V vs Nicaragua 120V).

Why is this different?

Malta uses 230V electricity, while Nicaragua uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
