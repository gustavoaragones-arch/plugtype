Can you use your devices in Hong Kong?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Hong Kong—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Hong Kong?

Yes — if your plug type from Guatemala does not match the outlets in Hong Kong, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types G in Hong Kong—they are not always the same as in Guatemala.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Hong Kong; plug types used there differ from Guatemala. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Guatemala uses 120V electricity, while Hong Kong uses 220V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
