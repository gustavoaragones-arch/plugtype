Can you use your devices in China?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in China with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for China?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in China, you will still need a travel adapter.

Outlets in China are built for plug types A C I; compare those with what you use in Montenegro.

Compatibility summary

Your Montenegro plug may fit in China outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Montenegro plug types: C F

Both countries share at least one plug type (C F and A C I), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Montenegro: 230V, 50Hz

China: 220V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Montenegro uses 230V electricity, while China uses 220V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
