Can you use your devices in Jamaica?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Jamaica once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Jamaica?

Yes — if your plug type from South Africa does not match the outlets in Jamaica, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Jamaica are built for plug types A B; compare those with what you use in South Africa.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Jamaica; plug types used there differ from South Africa. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

South Africa: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (South Africa 230V vs Jamaica 110V).

Why is this different?

South Africa uses 230V electricity, while Jamaica uses 110V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
