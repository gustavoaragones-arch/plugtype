Can you use your devices in Yemen?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Yemen—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Yemen?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Yemen, you will still need a travel adapter.

Yemen uses plug types C D G, which may differ from Serbia.

Compatibility summary

Your Serbia plug may fit in Yemen outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Serbia: 230V, 50Hz

Yemen: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Serbia uses 230V electricity, while Yemen uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
