Can you use your devices in Panama?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Panama—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Panama?

Yes — if your plug type from Saint Martin does not match the outlets in Panama, you will need a travel adapter.

Panama uses plug types A B, which may differ from Saint Martin.

Why is this different?

Saint Martin uses 230V electricity, while Panama uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
