Can you use your devices in Puerto Rico?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Puerto Rico—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Puerto Rico?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Puerto Rico, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types A B in Puerto Rico—they are not always the same as in Ecuador.

Compatibility summary

Your Ecuador plug may fit in Puerto Rico outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Why is this different?

Ecuador uses 120V electricity, while Puerto Rico uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
