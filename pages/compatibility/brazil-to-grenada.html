Can you use your devices in Grenada?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Grenada once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Grenada?

Yes — if your plug type from Brazil does not match the outlets in Grenada, you will need a travel adapter.

Grenada uses plug types G, which may differ from Brazil.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Grenada; plug types used there differ from Brazil. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Brazil uses 127V electricity, while Grenada uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
