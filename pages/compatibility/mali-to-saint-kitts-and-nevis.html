Can you use your devices in Saint Kitts and Nevis?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Saint Kitts and Nevis—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Kitts and Nevis?

Yes — if your plug type from Mali does not match the outlets in Saint Kitts and Nevis, you will need a travel adapter.

Saint Kitts and Nevis uses plug types A B G, which may differ from Mali.

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Mali uses 230V electricity, while Saint Kitts and Nevis uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
