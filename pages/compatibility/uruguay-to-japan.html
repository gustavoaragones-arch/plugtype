Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Japan; plug types used there differ from Uruguay. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Uruguay plug types: C F L

Japan plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Uruguay (C F L) and Japan (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Uruguay: 230V, 50Hz

Japan: 100V, 50Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Uruguay 230V vs Japan 100V).

Why is this different?

Uruguay uses 230V electricity, while Japan uses 100V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
