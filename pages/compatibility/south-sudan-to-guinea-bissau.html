Can you use your devices in Guinea-Bissau?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Guinea-Bissau—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guinea-Bissau?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Guinea-Bissau, you will still need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Guinea-Bissau—they are not always the same as in South Sudan.

Compatibility summary

Your South Sudan plug may fit in Guinea-Bissau outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

South Sudan uses 230V electricity, while Guinea-Bissau uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
