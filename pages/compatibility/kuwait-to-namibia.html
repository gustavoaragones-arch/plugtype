Can you use your devices in Namibia?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Namibia—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Namibia?

Yes — if your plug type from Kuwait does not match the outlets in Namibia, you will need a travel adapter.

Namibia uses plug types C M N, which may differ from Kuwait.

Why is this different?

Kuwait uses 230V electricity, while Namibia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
