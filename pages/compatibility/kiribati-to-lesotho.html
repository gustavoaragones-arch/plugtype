Can you use your devices in Lesotho?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Lesotho once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Lesotho?

Yes — if your plug type from Kiribati does not match the outlets in Lesotho, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Lesotho are built for plug types M; compare those with what you use in Kiribati.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Lesotho; plug types used there differ from Kiribati.

Voltage comparison

Kiribati: 240V, 50Hz

Lesotho: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Kiribati uses 240V electricity, while Lesotho uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
