Can you use your devices in Indonesia?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Indonesia once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Indonesia?

Yes — if your plug type from Palau does not match the outlets in Indonesia, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C F in Indonesia—they are not always the same as in Palau.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Indonesia; plug types used there differ from Palau. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Palau uses 120V electricity, while Indonesia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
