Can you use your devices in Guatemala?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Guatemala with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guatemala?

Yes — if your plug type from France does not match the outlets in Guatemala, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types A B in Guatemala—they are not always the same as in France.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Guatemala; plug types used there differ from France. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

France uses 230V electricity, while Guatemala uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
