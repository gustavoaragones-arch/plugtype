Can you use your devices in Saint Kitts and Nevis?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Saint Kitts and Nevis once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Kitts and Nevis?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Saint Kitts and Nevis, you will still need a travel adapter.

Saint Kitts and Nevis uses plug types A B G, which may differ from Zambia.

Compatibility summary

Your Zambia plug may fit in Saint Kitts and Nevis outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Zambia: 230V, 50Hz

Saint Kitts and Nevis: 230V, 60Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Zambia uses 230V electricity, while Saint Kitts and Nevis uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
