Can you use your devices in North Macedonia?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in North Macedonia—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for North Macedonia?

Yes — if your plug type from Oman does not match the outlets in North Macedonia, you will need a travel adapter.

North Macedonia uses plug types C F, which may differ from Oman.

Oman: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Oman uses 230V electricity, while North Macedonia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
