Can you use your devices in Argentina?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Argentina—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Argentina?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Argentina, you will still need a travel adapter.

Argentina uses plug types C I, which may differ from Georgia.

Compatibility summary

Your Georgia plug may fit in Argentina outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Plug comparison

Georgia plug types: C F

Argentina plug types: C I

Both countries share at least one plug type (C F and C I), so some plugs may work without an adapter. Check your appliance.

Voltage comparison

Georgia: 230V, 50Hz

Argentina: 220V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Georgia uses 230V electricity, while Argentina uses 220V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
