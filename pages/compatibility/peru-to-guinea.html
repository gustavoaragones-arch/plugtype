Can you use your devices in Guinea?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Guinea with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Guinea?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Guinea, you will still need a travel adapter.

Guinea uses plug types C F, which may differ from Peru.

Compatibility summary

Your Peru plug may fit in Guinea outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

Peru: 230V, 60Hz

Guinea: 230V, 50Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

Peru uses 230V electricity, while Guinea uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
