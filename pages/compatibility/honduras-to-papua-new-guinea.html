Can you use your devices in Papua New Guinea?

Devices rated 100–240V are widely compatible in Papua New Guinea—you typically only need the right plug adapter for the outlet. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Papua New Guinea?

Yes — if your plug type from Honduras does not match the outlets in Papua New Guinea, you will need a travel adapter.

Papua New Guinea uses plug types I, which may differ from Honduras.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Papua New Guinea; plug types used there differ from Honduras. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Honduras uses 120V electricity, while Papua New Guinea uses 240V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
