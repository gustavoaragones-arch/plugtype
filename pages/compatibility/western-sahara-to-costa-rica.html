Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Costa Rica; plug types used there differ from Western Sahara. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Plug comparison

Western Sahara plug types: C E

Costa Rica plug types: A B

There is no common plug type between Western Sahara (C E) and Costa Rica (A B). You need a travel adapter.

Voltage comparison

Western Sahara: 230V, 50Hz

Costa Rica: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage converter recommended (Western Sahara 230V vs Costa Rica 120V).

Why is this different?

Western Sahara uses 230V electricity, while Costa Rica uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Learn more: Why plug types differ worldwide

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
