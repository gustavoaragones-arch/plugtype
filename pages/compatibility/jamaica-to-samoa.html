Can you use your devices in Samoa?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Samoa with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Samoa?

Yes — if your plug type from Jamaica does not match the outlets in Samoa, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Samoa are built for plug types I; compare those with what you use in Jamaica.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Samoa; plug types used there differ from Jamaica. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Jamaica uses 110V electricity, while Samoa uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
