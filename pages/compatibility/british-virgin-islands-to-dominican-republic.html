Can you use your devices in Dominican Republic?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Dominican Republic with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Dominican Republic?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Dominican Republic, you will still need a travel adapter.

Dominican Republic uses plug types A B, which may differ from British Virgin Islands.

Your British Virgin Islands plug may fit in Dominican Republic outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Voltage comparison

British Virgin Islands: 110V, 60Hz

Dominican Republic: 120V, 60Hz

Voltage difference is within a range many devices tolerate; check your appliance label.

Why is this different?

British Virgin Islands uses 110V electricity, while Dominican Republic uses 120V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
