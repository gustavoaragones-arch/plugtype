Can you use your devices in Tanzania?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Tanzania with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Tanzania?

You might not need a plug shape adapter if your device already uses a type common to both countries. If your plug does not match any outlet type in Tanzania, you will still need a travel adapter.

Tanzania uses plug types G, which may differ from Ghana.

Compatibility summary

Your Ghana plug may fit in Tanzania outlets, as both use at least one common plug type.

Why is this different?

Ghana uses 230V electricity, while Tanzania uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
