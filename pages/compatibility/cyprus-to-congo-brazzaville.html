Can you use your devices in Republic of the Congo?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Republic of the Congo once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Republic of the Congo?

Yes — if your plug type from Cyprus does not match the outlets in Republic of the Congo, you will need a travel adapter.

Expect plug types C E in Republic of the Congo—they are not always the same as in Cyprus.

Why is this different?

Cyprus uses 230V electricity, while Republic of the Congo uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
