Can you use your devices in Netherlands?

If your device supports 100–240V, it will work in Netherlands with just a plug adapter when the socket shape differs. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Netherlands?

Yes — if your plug type from Bermuda does not match the outlets in Netherlands, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Netherlands are built for plug types C F; compare those with what you use in Bermuda.

Compatibility summary

You will need a travel adapter in Netherlands; plug types used there differ from Bermuda. Voltage differs by more than 20% — a voltage converter may be needed for some appliances.

Why is this different?

Bermuda uses 120V electricity, while Netherlands uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

Because the voltage difference is significant, some devices may require a voltage converter.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
