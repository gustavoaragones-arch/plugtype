Can you use your devices in Saint Lucia?

Dual-voltage gear (often labeled 100–240V) can run in Saint Lucia once you have a matching plug or adapter. Most modern electronics like phones and laptops are dual voltage.

If your device is single voltage (for example 120V only), you may need a voltage converter.

Do you need a plug adapter for Saint Lucia?

Yes — if your plug type from Serbia does not match the outlets in Saint Lucia, you will need a travel adapter.

Outlets in Saint Lucia are built for plug types G; compare those with what you use in Serbia.

Why is this different?

Serbia uses 230V electricity, while Saint Lucia uses 230V. These electrical systems developed independently, which is why both voltage and plug types differ.

Plug adapter vs voltage converter

Plug adapter: Allows your plug to fit into a different socket. It does not change voltage.

Voltage converter: Changes the electrical voltage to match your device requirements.

In most cases, you will only need a plug adapter for this route.

Important: Plug adapters do not convert voltage.

Always check your device label (e.g. 100–240V).
