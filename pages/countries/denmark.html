What plug types are used in Denmark?

Denmark uses plug types C and K. These plug types determine which devices can be connected directly.

Voltage and frequency in Denmark

The voltage in Denmark is 230V with a frequency of 50Hz.

If your device is not compatible with this voltage, you may need a converter.

Why plug types and voltage differ in Denmark

Electrical systems in different countries were developed independently, which is why plug shapes and voltage standards vary. Denmark uses 230V and plug types C K, which may differ from other regions.

Do you need a travel adapter for Denmark?

If your device plug does not match types C and K, you will need a plug adapter.

Can you use your devices in Denmark?
