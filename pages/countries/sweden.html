What plug types are used in Sweden?

Sweden uses plug types C and F. These plug types determine which devices can be connected directly.

Voltage and frequency in Sweden

The voltage in Sweden is 230V with a frequency of 50Hz.

If your device is not compatible with this voltage, you may need a converter.

Why plug types and voltage differ in Sweden

Electrical systems in different countries were developed independently, which is why plug shapes and voltage standards vary. Sweden uses 230V and plug types C F, which may differ from other regions.

Do you need a travel adapter for Sweden?
