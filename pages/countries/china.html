What plug types are used in China?

China uses plug types A, C, and I. These plug types determine which devices can be connected directly.

Voltage and frequency in China

The voltage in China is 220V with a frequency of 50Hz.

If your device is not compatible with this voltage, you may need a converter.

Why plug types and voltage differ in China

Electrical systems in different countries were developed independently, which is why plug shapes and voltage standards vary. China uses 220V and plug types A C I, which may differ from other regions.

Do you need a travel adapter for China?

If your device plug does not match types A, C, and I, you will need a plug adapter.

Can you use your devices in China?

Most modern electronics support 100–240V and will work with a plug adapter.
