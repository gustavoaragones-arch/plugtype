Plug adapter vs voltage converter: what’s the difference?

← Compatibility tool

A plug adapter allows your device to fit into a different socket shape, but it does not change voltage.

A voltage converter changes the electrical voltage, which is necessary when traveling between countries with different voltage systems.

Most modern electronics support dual voltage (100–240V), but high-power devices may require a converter.
