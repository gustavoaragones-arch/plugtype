What plug types are used in Finland?

Finland uses plug types C and F. These plug types determine which devices can be connected directly.

Voltage and frequency in Finland

The voltage in Finland is 230V with a frequency of 50Hz.

If your device is not compatible with this voltage, you may need a converter.

Why plug types and voltage differ in Finland

Electrical systems in different countries were developed independently, which is why plug shapes and voltage standards vary. Finland uses 230V and plug types C F, which may differ from other regions.

Do you need a travel adapter for Finland?

If your device plug does not match types C and F, you will need a plug adapter.

Can you use your devices in Finland?
