What plug types are used in Japan?

If your device is not compatible with this voltage, you may need a converter.

Why plug types and voltage differ in Japan

Electrical systems in different countries were developed independently, which is why plug shapes and voltage standards vary. Japan uses 100V and plug types A B, which may differ from other regions.
